## Visual DNA, defined before implementation
- Authority through deliberate proportions, not claims of superiority.
- Precision through aligned text, controlled measures and one recurring cut.
- Calm through a restricted palette and motion that finishes.
- Human control: the text and final decision remain legible beside the AI's contribution.
- Material connection: the page behaves like an open professional folio, not a dashboard costume.
- Distinctiveness through the combined black field, stepped folio silhouette and compact lettering; no color is presumed ownable by itself.
- Honest evidence: a labeled editing example and the actual application capture; no invented results, customers or certifications.
